# SWYNEX-Biological-Dataset-Selection
Selection and documentation of a public biological gene-expression dataset for SWYNEX internship Task 1.
## Dataset 
Differential expression analysis of human brain samples with Alzheimer disease versus healthy control samples
## Data Source
NCBI GEO
## Organism
*Homo sapiens*
## Data type 
gene expression
## Technology used
Microarray
## Dataset size
36 (Originally included data from 40 samples, but data from 4 arrays for the final dataset).
## Research question 
What differences in gene expression can be observed between Alzheimer's disease and healthy brain samples?
## Why is selected this dataset
I selected this dataset to explore a biological dataset outside my usual plant-focused work. Gene expression in humans was an interesting area for me to explore and would give me exposure to a different type of biological data.
